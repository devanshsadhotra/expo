Let’s get your local development environment ready so you can start building and running your Expo project on Android or iOS.

## Where do you plan to develop?

To get the most accurate experience, we recommend using a real device. This ensures you see your app the way your users will.

## How would you like to run your project?

You have two options:
- **Expo Go** — A quick and easy way to preview your project using the Expo Go app.
- **Development build** — A custom build of your app that includes Expo developer tools and allows more flexibility.
